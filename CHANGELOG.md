# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Expected wishlist cost at the next seasonal Steam sale, for your own or any public wishlist.
- Discounts from IsThereAnyDeal, regular price from the Steam store in the viewer's region and currency.
- Price region can be changed from the panel.
- Click a game to leave it out of the total.
- Sorting of the per-game list by name, discount or price.
- List of games that are not counted, with the reason for each.
- Progress bar with a time estimate under the IsThereAnyDeal rate limit.
- 13 interface languages, picked from the Steam language, for the panel and the settings page.
